Welcome to my GitHub profile! I am interested in the intersection of **Mathematics, Artificial Intelligence, and Physical Systems**, with a particular focus on developing mathematically grounded and computationally efficient learning algorithms.

My long-term goal is to enable robots to reconstruct and perceive the physical fields of their surrounding environments with high fidelity from sparse sensor observations, extending robotic perception beyond conventional visual and geometric information.

## 🎯 Research Interests

- **Scientific Machine Learning (SciML):** Neural operators, physics-informed learning, and data-driven modeling of partial differential equations (PDEs).
- **Physical Field Reconstruction:** High-fidelity reconstruction and prediction of continuous physical fields from sparse, noisy, and incomplete observations.
- **Embodied AI & Robotic Perception:** Exploring how robots can perceive and understand their surrounding physical environments beyond conventional visual perception.
- **Spatiotemporal Learning:** Transformers, operator learning, and efficient modeling of complex physical dynamics.
- **Mathematical Foundations of AI:** Functional analysis, real analysis, approximation theory, and mathematical principles underlying deep learning.
- **Large Language Models:** Transformer architectures, representation learning, and their potential applications in scientific and physical systems.

## 🔭 Currently Working On

- Exploring efficient learning algorithms for physical field reconstruction and prediction.
- Studying neural operators and mathematical methods for learning PDE-governed systems.
- Investigating mathematical structures that improve model accuracy, stability, and computational efficiency.
- Exploring connections between scientific machine learning and embodied intelligence.

## 🌱 Long-Term Vision
